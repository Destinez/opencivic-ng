# Independent evidence decisions

Accepted 15 November 2026. Owner: Obaro Destiny Udurie.

A reviewer cannot claim or decide a sheet they submitted. Each reviewer has one decision per sheet. L0 observers require two independent approvals and another observer's corroboration before their sheet can support a case. L1 and higher require one sheet review.

A reference transcriber cannot verify the same reference. A case opener cannot confirm the case. Confirmation rechecks current evidence, records the independent decision and publishes in one transaction. Published case facts and evidence snapshots become immutable.

Retraction requires two different admins and a visible note; the original evidence and case history remain. Each transition appends to the audit hash chain. A failed audit write rolls back the associated decision.

This costs reviewer time but keeps one person from publishing a document discrepancy without another check. It reduces misinformation risk without treating reviewer agreement as an official electoral finding.
